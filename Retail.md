Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using MySQL and Microsoft Power BI to identify sales trends, product performance, and business insights.

Tools Used

- MySQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization

Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Total Stores

Dashboard Visualizations

- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by City
- Sales by Category
- Interactive City and Order Status Slicers

Key Business Insight

Laptop is the highest-selling product among the top five products displayed in the dashboard.

Project Objective

To analyze retail sales performance and present useful business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
